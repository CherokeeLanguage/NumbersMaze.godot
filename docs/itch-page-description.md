# itch.io page description — Cherokee Numbers Maze

<!-- the description body begins at the cut line -->

---

<p>Learn the Cherokee numbers — ᏣᎳᎩ ᏗᏎᏍᏗ — by playing a maze game.</p>

<p>Cherokee Numbers Maze is a simple maze game that helps you learn the
Cherokee numbers. Wander around the maze and collect dice together that
match the challenge you were given — then explode them. Complete all of a
level's challenges and you move on to the next, larger level with more
numbers to learn.</p>

<p><strong>Gamepad:</strong> left stick or D-pad to move · <strong>[A]</strong> fireball · <strong>[X]</strong> pick up dice · <strong>[Y]</strong> drop dice</p>

<p><strong>Keyboard:</strong> arrow keys to move · <strong>[F]</strong>, <strong>[Space]</strong>, or <strong>[Enter]</strong> fireball · <strong>[D]</strong> pick up dice · <strong>[S]</strong> drop dice</p>

<p><strong>Platforms:</strong> play it right here in your browser, or download builds for Windows, macOS, Linux, and Android — every build is served by this itch page.</p>

<p><strong>Installing:</strong> the Android APK installs directly on your device — Android may ask you to allow installing apps from this source once. The Windows, macOS, and Linux builds are plain downloads: grab them and run.</p>
